The problem

Your help center is a museum.

Documentation gets written once. Then the product moves, and re-recording it was never anybody's job. So your screenshots describe a version of the product you stopped shipping — and your team covers the gap live, on calls, one customer at a time.

Your CSMs become the documentation.

The same six screens, explained live, again. It's the most expensive way to teach anything and it never scales past the team explaining it.

Docs decay silently.

Nothing breaks and no alert fires. You find out when a customer follows the old steps and lands somewhere that no longer exists.

Video is a sealed artifact.

Rename one menu and the whole recording is wrong. So re-recording costs what recording cost, and most teams just leave it.
